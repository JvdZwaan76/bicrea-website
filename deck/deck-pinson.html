Why Severed Minerals Matter on Solar & Wind
The problem your developers bring you

Severed-mineral owners can use the surface.
A solar array needs all of it.

In most states a severed-mineral owner may use as much of the surface as is reasonably necessary to reach the minerals. Texas’s El Paso Court of Appeals has held the accommodation doctrine governs solar-versus-mineral disputes (Lyle v. Midway Solar, 618 S.W.3d 857, Tex. App.—El Paso 2020, pet. denied). Title policies except severed minerals in Schedule B, and the array endorsements (ALTA 35.3, 36.9) insure improvements, not mineral title — so underwriters want the severance search done first.

What has to be found
Every severance, by depth and by product
Patent reservations, mineral deeds, fractional heirs, unreleased mineral mortgages, dormant interests — traced from sovereignty forward, not from the last vesting deed.
What has to be documented
An index an underwriter can work from
Chain, fractions, and curative items tied to recorded instruments, so waivers go to the right owners. In Lyle, waivers from people with no interest in the tract clouded the true owners’ title.
Where the time goes
Owner location
Surface-waiver campaigns routinely take the better part of a year — longer where heirs or dormant interests are involved. The research has to be front-loaded and the heirs found early — before the waiver campaign starts, not during it.

Scope of a BIC REA Mineral Title Report
What is — and is not — in the report

A documented analysis of the record,
built for your examiner.

It is
Research your counsel can build an opinion on
Current record ownership as reflected in the instruments examined · surface and mineral estates separated · undivided-interest fractions computed from the recorded language · encumbrances, leases, and mining claims · curative items identified, sourced, and categorized · a source index so every finding traces to an instrument · BLM and state records cross-referenced.
It is not
A legal opinion, a certified abstract, or title insurance
Drilling, division-order, and acquisition opinions are issued by independent licensed counsel engaged directly by the client; we deliver our research in the form counsel needs. We do not issue certified abstracts in states that license abstracting. Off-record matters are outside the scope of records-based research.
