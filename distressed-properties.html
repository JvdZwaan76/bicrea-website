For Florida Property Owners & Heirs

We buy difficult Florida properties — homes and land

Code-enforcement liens, inherited or unsettled title, back taxes, a vacant lot nobody in the family can carry. We buy as-is, for cash, and pay for the title work and the attorneys ourselves. No pressure and no clock from us — take your time and have someone you trust look it over.

What we buy

Properties the usual buyers walk away from

Code-enforcement and utility liens

Daily fines, demolition orders, lot-mowing and water liens, however old or large. We work through the city or county lien-settlement process ourselves, at our cost.

Inherited and heirs property

A parent or grandparent still on title, an estate never opened, several relatives with an interest. We hire and pay a Florida probate attorney and pay each heir cash for their interest in the estate.

Back taxes and vacant lots

Delinquent taxes, a lot that earns nothing and costs something every year, land after a house has come down. Tracing title back to the patent is our day job, so we can buy what others can’t.

How it works

Three steps, on your timeline

1

You tell us about it

Seven short questions on our intake form, or a phone call. We pull the county and city records ourselves — you don’t need paperwork to start.

2

We put an offer in writing

A plain written offer with what we pay and what we take on: liens, title work, the probate attorney’s fees, the city. Have your own attorney read it. We’re glad to talk with them directly.

3

You decide, whenever you’re ready

Clean cash purchase, as-is, closed through a Florida title agent or attorney. No sale-leasebacks, no repurchase options, nothing that leaves you tied to the property.
