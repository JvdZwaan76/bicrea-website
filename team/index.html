The non-negotiables (before every send)

  1. Verified address only. Website, LinkedIn, a reception call, or a proven pattern from that exact domain. Never guess a format. (Friday's failures: seamusfitzgerald@ / sfitzgerald@ / seamus.fitzgerald@ — all guessed, all bounced; the real contact at sm-energy turned out to be jblackburn@, not jordanblackburn@.)
  2. Zero attachments on first contact. Everything lives at bicrea.com/welcome — one link does the work.
  3. Plain text. No images in the body — and use the "Outreach — plain" signature, no logo image. Zoho counts a signature logo as an attachment, and it is our leading suspect for cold emails "arriving with issues" at strict receivers.
  4. One link maximum (the welcome page). Multiple links raise spam scores.
  5. Short. First email under ~130 words of body. Long cold emails get skimmed by humans and flagged by machines.
  6. Person, not inbox. Address a named individual with a first line only they could receive. No info@, no contact@.
  7. Subject lines: specific and calm. Never "Urgent," "Free," "Immediate," "Contract Attached," "Wire," "Guarantee," ALL CAPS, or exclamation marks.
  8. Critical mail gets a second channel. If it matters, confirm receipt by phone within two business days (that is the follow-up call, not a nag).
  9. Bounces go to Jasper same day. And check your own Spam folder weekly.

Subject: Title research support — [Company]

Body
Hi [First name], [One specific sentence about them — a project, permit approval, filing, or announcement. This line is why the email gets read.] I'm [Full name], [Title] of BIC REA LLC. We provide comprehensive title research — surface, mineral, and leasehold — with GIS-mapped deliverables, committed timelines, and attorney opinion coverage coordinated through independent licensed counsel, confirmed per state at engagement. We mobilize nationwide. If title support would help on a current project, our capabilities overview is here: bicrea.com/welcome Happy to send a sample title report or set up a short call.

Template 2 — The bump (one only, ~1 week later)

Subject: "Re:" on the original thread — do not start a new one.

[First name] — floating this back up once in case the timing was off. If title capacity isn't a need right now, no reply required; we'll leave you be. If it ever is: bicrea.com/welcome or my line below. [Plain signature]

After the bump: 90 days of silence before that contact can be touched again. Log the date in the Outreach Tracker.

Template 3 — Reply when someone reports a problem ("your email had issues")

[First name] — thank you for letting me know, and apologies that it arrived that way. I'd genuinely like to fix whatever went wrong on our end: if you can tell me what you saw (garbled formatting, a blocked image, anything at all), it goes straight to our setup. The short version of who we are: overflow title support — comprehensive research across surface, mineral, and leasehold interests, attorney-issued opinions available, nationwide travel. Full details: bicrea.com/welcome [Plain signature]

Language rules (match the website exactly)

Say: comprehensive title research · surface, mineral, and leasehold interests · attorney-issued opinion coverage "coordinated through independent licensed counsel; availability confirmed per state at engagement" · GIS-mapped deliverables · source index · committed timelines, transparent pricing · nationwide travel.

Never say: "mineral title research firm" (old identity) · "our attorney network" or "our attorneys" (compliance: prohibited until a first fee agreement is signed) · "$2.1B" or any luxury-era language · price figures of any kind in cold email · "guaranteed."

Quick checklist (tape this to the monitor)

  • Address verified (not guessed)
  • Named person
  • Personalized first line
  • Under 130 words
  • Plain text, "Outreach — plain" signature
  • Zero attachments
  • One link (welcome page)
  • Calm subject
  • Logged in Outreach Tracker
